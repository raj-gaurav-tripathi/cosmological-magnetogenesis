# cosmological-magnetogenesis

This repository contains worked-out study notes on cosmological magnetogenesis from ultralight dark matter, focusing on two related 2026 papers:

- Late Time Magnetogenesis from Ultralight Scalar Dark Matter
- ALP Dark Matter, Cosmological Magnetic Fields and the Direct Collapse Black Hole Formation Scenario

The notes expand the derivations, explain the physical motivation, and organize the results into a more readable study format.

## Contents

- `Magnetogenisis_UL-Scalar DM.pdf`
  - Worked-out notes on ultralight scalar dark matter and late-time magnetogenesis.
- `Magnetogenisis_form_ALP-DM.pdf`
  - Worked-out notes on ALP dark matter, cosmological magnetic fields, secondary photon production, and DCBH formation.

## Topics covered

- FRW setup and conformal-time formulation
- Gauge-field equations of motion
- Tachyonic instability and resonance
- Magnetic field generation from dark matter
- Secondary photon production
- Lyman-Werner flux and DCBH criterion
- Observational constraints and parameter space

## Purpose

These notes are meant as a study companion and derivation guide for the referenced papers.

## References

- V. Kamali and R. Brandenberger, *Late Time Magnetogenesis from Ultralight Scalar Dark Matter* (2026)
- A. Kushwaha and R. Brandenberger, *ALP Dark Matter, Cosmological Magnetic Fields and the Direct Collapse Black Hole Formation Scenario* (2026)
